Dr. Aris Thorne (ID: ZX12)

← Back to Murder Mystery

The Health Associate

Cornelius's private "wellness consultant" who managed his diet, supplements, and longevity treatments.

His Secret

He isn't actually a medical doctor; his degree is from a non-accredited online university. He's been prescribing Cornelius placebos and dangerous unapproved supplements.

Background

Aris Thorne presented himself as a revolutionary in the field of life extension and bio-hacking. He charmed his way into Cornelius's inner circle, promising the aging billionaire decades of extra life through his "proprietary protocols." In reality, he's a con artist with a background in multi-level marketing schemes.

Motive

Cornelius recently had his bloodwork analyzed by a legitimate lab and discovered that Thorne's "exclusive" life-extension treatments were just sugar pills and caffeine. Cornelius was furious and was preparing to sue Thorne for fraud and have him arrested.

Opportunity

Thorne had unrestricted access to Cornelius's food and drink, as he prepared all of his "nutritional supplements." He was in the kitchen mixing a "vitality blend" for Cornelius shortly before the death.

Alibi

Claims he was in the kitchen preparing Cornelius's evening "vitality blend" and organizing his supplement case.
